How it works

Book it tonight, ride it tomorrow

No experience needed. Our team walks every rider through the controls and the route before the throttle goes down.

Reserve online

Pick your adventure and a date. Reservations are required for every trip on the water, and weekends go first.

Arrive early

Jet ski riders check in 30 minutes before start time. Boat renters need 10. Waivers, life jackets, and a quick briefing happen here.

Go find dolphins

Follow your guide out past the breakwater. Common dolphins travel in pods of hundreds here, and the whales pass through most of the year.

Know before you go

The short list

  • 18+ to drive a jet ski. Passengers must be 7 or older. Bring a valid ID.
  • Arrive early. 30 minutes before a jet ski start, 10 minutes before a boat rental.
  • Dogs are welcome on the electric boats. Bring a towel for the ride home.
  • Harbor fees are added at checkout. Reservations are required for everything on the water.
  • No experience needed. Every rider gets a briefing and a guide on the water.
