The boat

Your own floating living room

Cruise up and down Channel Islands Harbor with family and friends, and meet a few sea lions along the way. The Duffy tops out at 5 mph, so nobody needs boating experience. We hand you a harbor map and walk you through the controls before you leave the dock.

  • Seats 10 comfortably, 12 maximum. Kids count.
  • Full canopy and window enclosures for the breezy evenings
  • Radio and Bluetooth for your own playlist
  • Bring dinner, drinks, and a cooler. Bring the dog, too.

Requirements

Before you cast off

  • The driver must be 18 or older and bring a valid driver's license and a major credit card to sign the rental agreement.
  • Arrive 10 minutes early for the walkthrough and the harbor map.
  • Life jackets are under the seats for every passenger. Kids 12 and under wear theirs by law, and we fit them before boarding.
  • Harbor fees are added at checkout.

Good to know

Make a night of it

  • Bring your playlist. The onboard system takes any Bluetooth device, or tune the radio.
  • Dogs ride free. A towel for the seats is appreciated.
  • Sunset is the move. The 2 hour evening cruise runs Friday through Sunday from 6 to 8 pm.
  • Sea lions live here. You'll usually find a few hauled out on the docks near the harbor mouth.
