Channel Islands dolphin adventures

Dolphins & whales, up close

The Santa Barbara Channel is one of the richest stretches of ocean on the West Coast. Pods of common dolphins run in the hundreds here, and the whales pass through most of the year.

What you'll see

Pods in the hundreds

Long-beaked and short-beaked common dolphins live in the channel year-round and travel in huge groups. On a good day they run alongside the skis for the whole ride. Bottlenose dolphins work the shoreline, and sea lions hang out at the harbor mouth.

Whales move through on a schedule. Gray whales migrate past from December through April. Humpbacks and blue whales feed in the channel through the summer. They're wild animals and we never promise a sighting, but this is one of the best places in California to try.
