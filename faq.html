General

Getting here

Where are you located?

We're 30 minutes north of Malibu in Channel Islands Harbor, at Marine Emporium Landing. The address is 3600 S. Harbor Blvd., Oxnard, CA 93035. Look for the Boat Rentals kiosk on the water side of the plaza.

Is parking available?

Yes, and it's free.

What time should we arrive for our reservation?

Please arrive 30 minutes before your reservation time for jet skis, and 10 minutes before for electric boats. That covers waivers, life jackets, and the briefing.

Do I need a reservation?

Yes for everything on the water. Bicycles are first come, first served.

Book online or call (805) 910-7257.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome on the electric boats. Bring a towel for the seats.

Jet skis

Riding

How much does it cost to jet ski?

The 60-minute dolphin adventure starts at $155 per jet ski and the 90-minute adventure starts at $226.50, plus harbor fees. Each ski carries a driver and one passenger. For longer rentals, call (805) 910-7257.

Do I need to put down a deposit to rent a jet ski?

Yes. We require a $550 damage deposit, cash or card. Cash deposits are returned after your rental. Card deposits can take up to five business days to release.

Can you accommodate large groups on the jet skis?

Absolutely. We have up to 12 jet skis available, and each can carry two people. The person driving each jet ski needs to bring their ID and the $550 damage deposit, cash or card.

What is the minimum age to rent a jet ski?

You must be at least 18 years old with a driver's license or valid ID to drive. Passengers must be at least 7, and passengers under 18 ride with a parent or guardian. Parents sign a liability waiver for minor passengers.

How many people can ride a jet ski?

One driver and one passenger. Only one person operates the jet ski during the rental. Passengers stay passengers.

I've never gone jet skiing before. Is that a problem?

Not at all. One of our instructors will show you everything you need to know before you leave the dock, and a guide leads the ride.

What should I wear to go jet skiing?

In the summer, a bathing suit or board shorts, and ride barefoot. We have changing facilities on site. Bring a towel and sunscreen. In the cooler months we rent spring suits, known as shorties, for $10 each.

Are life jackets provided?

Coast Guard approved life jackets are provided to all riders at no additional cost.

Are your jet skis on a trailer?

No. Our jet skis are in the water and ready to go.

How fast are your jet skis?

They can reach speeds of up to 45 mph on the open ocean, but we suggest only going as fast as feels safe to you. It's not the speed, it's the journey.

What if I fall off the jet ski?

Climb back on from the back of the jet ski. Your life jacket keeps you up, and your guide is right there.

Electric boats

Cruising

How many people can fit on each Duffy electric boat?

Our boats seat up to 10 people comfortably. The maximum permitted is 12. These numbers apply to passengers of all ages, so kids count.

What is the minimum age to drive a Duffy electric boat?

The person signing the rental agreement and operating the boat must be 18 or older, and will need to bring a valid driver's license and a major credit card.

Are the Duffy electric boats easy to drive?

As easy as driving a golf cart, with speeds maxing out at 5 mph. We provide a detailed map of Channel Islands Harbor and go over how to drive the boat and where to go before you depart.

Are life jackets provided?

US Coast Guard approved life jackets are available for all passengers and are kept underneath the boat's seats. Children 12 and under are legally required to wear one, and we fit them before boarding.

Can we listen to music on the boat?

Yes. Tune in to the radio or connect any Bluetooth device to the boat's sound system.

Can we bring food and drinks on the boat?

You are welcome to bring any food or beverage. Feel free to bring a cooler, too.

When are the evening rentals?

Two-hour sunset cruises run Friday through Sunday from 6 to 8 pm. Reservations are required and they sell out first.
