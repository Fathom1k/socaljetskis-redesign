The fleet

In the water, ready to go

No trailers, no boat ramp, no waiting. Our Sea-Doos sit at the dock at Marine Emporium Landing. You check in, gear up, get your briefing, and step on.

  • Up to 12 jet skis, each with room for a driver and one passenger
  • Top speed around 45 mph on open ocean. Go as fast as feels right to you.
  • Coast Guard approved life jackets for every rider, included
  • Changing facilities on site. Shorty wetsuits available for $10 in the cooler months.

Requirements

Who can ride

  • Drivers must be 18 or older with a valid driver's license or ID. Only one person operates each jet ski during the rental.
  • Passengers must be 7 or older. Riders under 18 ride with a parent or guardian, who signs a liability waiver for them.
  • $550 damage deposit, cash or card. Cash deposits come back after your ride. Card holds can take up to five business days to release.
  • Arrive 30 minutes early. Check-in, waivers, life jackets, and the safety briefing all happen before your start time.

What to bring

Pack light

  • Swimsuit or board shorts, and ride barefoot. In summer that's all you need.
  • Towel and sunscreen. You will get wet. That's the point.
  • Life jackets are on us. Coast Guard approved, every size, no extra charge.
  • Want longer than 90 minutes? Call (805) 910-7257 and we'll set up a custom rental.
